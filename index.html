THE BLOCK is a single street corner rendered six times over — 1945, 1965, 1985, 2005, 2025 and 2055 — and every version is generated in code at load time. No downloaded models, no photographs, no audio files: the bricks, the neon, the sedans, the pedestrians, the swing band on the radio and the hum of a 2055 delivery drone are all synthesised in your browser.

The buildings persist across time. The theatre becomes a cinema becomes a video store becomes a gym becomes an archive. Paint layers up and peels back. Look for the details that survive all six eras — somebody left something in the wall in 1945.
